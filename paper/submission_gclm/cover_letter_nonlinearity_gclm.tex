Dear Editors of \emph{Nonlinearity},

Please find enclosed the anonymized manuscript \emph{``Stability Spectra, Exact Two-Gamma Conjugation, and Zero-Analyticity Finite-Sobolev Nonlinear Closure for the Singular Self-Similar Profiles of the Generalized CLM Model''} for consideration as a Research Paper in \emph{Nonlinearity} under double-anonymous peer review.

Following the classic introduction of the generalized Constantin--Lax--Majda (gCLM) model in \emph{Nonlinearity} (Okamoto--Sakajo--Wunsch, 2008) and the recent construction of explicit half-line outer singular self-similar blowup profiles for $a < 0$ by Huang--Tong--Wang (\texttt{arXiv:2603.25104v4}, 2026), the linear stability spectra, Mellin--Carleman operator structure, and nonlinear stability of these singular profiles remained open. In this manuscript we:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Uncover a second explicit half-line singular family (\emph{Branch~II}), $\bar\Omega_{\mathrm{II},a}(X) = \frac{1}{a}(X-1)^{-1/2}\mathbf{1}_{\{X>1\}}$, valid for every $a < 0$ with $(\bar c_l, \bar c_\omega, \gamma) = (2,-1,2)$, which intersects Huang--Tong--Wang's Branch~I transcritically at $a = -1$ with an exact exchange of partner eigenmodes and interpolates continuously to the singular 1D Hou--Luo profile as $a \to -\infty$.
  \item Compute the linear stability spectra $\lambda(a)$ across $a \in [-2.5, -0.1]$ in singularity-adapted logarithmic and characteristic coordinates using second-order upwind discretization, certifying $\#\{\operatorname{Re}\lambda > 0\} = 0$ for smooth relative perturbations and diagnosing spurious central-difference Nyquist edge artifacts.
  \item Introduce an edge-amplitude-pinning dynamic-rescaling gauge (\emph{Gauge~(c)}) that eliminates the $\delta(X-1)$ distributional defect identically and yields four rigorous theorems: exact two-Gamma Mellin conjugation on slit-plane Hardy space (Theorem~1), computer-free rational-polynomial $H^2_{\sigma_-}\cap H^3_{5/8}$ coercivity for all $a \in [-2,0)$ (Theorem~2), unconditional global existence, uniqueness, and exponential stability in pure finite Sobolev space requiring zero analyticity via weighted viscous regularization (Theorem~3 and Appendix~A), and an elementary three-line $H^1_{\sigma_-}\cap H^1_{\sigma_+}$ coercivity proof for Huang--Tong--Wang's Branch~I across all $a \in [-1,0)$ (Theorem~4).
  \item Explain nonlinear branch selection across $a = -1$ via an $L^2$-criticality edge-flux mechanism confirmed by WENO5/SSP-RK3 dynamic-rescaling DNS.
\end{enumerate}

All finite algebraic positivity certificates, weight-splitting identities, and Appendix~A bootstrap inequalities are machine-checked in Lean~4.29 / Mathlib (\texttt{0~sorry}, standard axioms only), and all continuous spectral, PDE, and DNS claims are reproduced by four standalone Python verification gates openly archived (for editorial inspection and upon publication) at \url{https://github.com/Brsanch/singular-blowup-1d} (branch \texttt{gclm/singular-stability}, tag \texttt{v1.1-gclm-submission-ready}).
