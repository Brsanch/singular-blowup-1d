## 1. Statement of the Theorem

Consider the 1D Hou–Luo dynamic-rescaling system `(eq:dyn)` in the $\sqrt{v}$ $\Theta$-cusp gauge $A \equiv B \equiv \xi \equiv 1$, $c_l(\tau) = 2 - V_G(\tau)$, $c_\omega(\tau) = -c_l(\tau)/2$ ($c_l + 2c_\omega \equiv 0$):
$$\Omega(X,\tau) = (X-1)^{-1/2}\bigl(1 + G(X-1,\tau)\bigr)\mathbf{1}_{X>1}, \qquad \Theta(X,\tau) = \bigl(\tfrac{\pi}{2} + \eta(X-1,\tau)\bigr)\mathbf{1}_{X>1},$$
where on $v = X - 1 > 0$, $(G, \eta)$ satisfies the exact nonlinear coupled system
$$\begin{cases}
G_\tau + \bigl(2 - V_G(\tau) + QG\bigr)\,v G_v - \dfrac{1}{2}(QG)(1 + G) = \sqrt{v}\,\eta_v, \\[6pt]
\eta_\tau + \bigl(2 - V_G(\tau) + QG\bigr)\,v \eta_v = 0,
\end{cases} \tag{1.1}$$
with weak-edge compatibility $a(\tau) + D(\tau) \equiv 0$ ($a(\tau) = (QG)(0,\tau)$, $D(\tau) = \lim_{v\to 0^+}\eta(v,\tau)/\sqrt{v}$, matching $B(\tau) \equiv A(\tau)^2 = 1$ across $X = 1$).

### Function spaces & explicit $O(\varepsilon^2 w^{3/2})$ initial cusp
Fix $\rho_0 \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2}]$. Define the Fourier–Mellin weight $\widehat{\langle D\rangle^r F}(\mu + i\xi) := (1 + \xi^2)^{r/2}\widehat F(\mu + i\xi)$, the boundary-trace Hardy–Mellin norm $\|F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, \mu}^2 := \int_{\mathbb{R}} e^{-2\mu x}(|F(e^{x+i\rho})|^2 + |F(e^{x-i\rho})|^2)\,dx = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}} \cosh(2\rho\xi)|\widehat F(\mu + i\xi)|^2\,d\xi$, and the two-line strip-analytic space (with **no** $a(G) = 0$ restriction, and $\langle D\rangle^{1/4}$ on $\sigma_2 = \frac{5}{8}$ so $\mathcal{N}_g$ has **exact integer** orders $k_1 = 2$ on $\sigma_1 = \frac{1}{4}$ and $k_2 = 3$ on $\sigma_2 = \frac{5}{8}$):
$$\mathcal{X}_\rho := H^2_\rho(\tfrac{1}{4}) \cap H^{1/4}_{L^2, \rho}(\tfrac{5}{8}), \qquad \|F\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}^2 := \|F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, 1/4}^2 + \|\langle D\rangle^{1/4} F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, 5/8}^2.$$
Given any initial profile $G_0 \in \mathcal{X}_{\rho_0}$ (including edge-analytic data with $a(G_0) \neq 0$), define the explicit scalar
$$c_0(G_0) := \tfrac{1}{4}\,a(G_0)^2 + \tfrac{1}{2}\,a(\mathcal{P}(G_0)), \qquad \mathcal{P}(G) := -(QG)DG + \tfrac{1}{2}(QG)G,$$
the explicit closed-form strip-analytic profile $\Phi_*(w) := \mathcal{N}_g[\frac{u}{u^2+1}](w) = \frac{1}{2w}e^{-1/\sqrt{w}}$ ($\widehat{\Phi_*}(m) = \Gamma(2m+2)$, holomorphic on $\operatorname{Re} m > -1$, lying in $\mathcal{X}_\rho$ for every $\rho < \pi$), and the **modified initial $\Theta$-cusp**:
$$\eta_0^{(\varepsilon)}(v) := \underbrace{-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\int_0^v y^{-1/2}(QG_0)(y)\,dy}_{\text{linear cusp } (- \varepsilon a_0\sqrt{v} + O(v^{3/2}))} \;+\; \underbrace{\varepsilon^2 \pi\,c_0(G_0)\int_0^v y^{-1/2}\Phi_*(y)\,dy}_{\text{explicit } O(\varepsilon^2 e^{-1/\sqrt{v}}) \text{ smoothing (zero }\sqrt{v}\text{ cusp!)}}. \tag{1.2}$$

> **Why the explicit $O(\varepsilon^2)$ term in (1.2) is included (resolving `A1`):** Without it ($W(\cdot,0) \equiv 0$), the defect $W(w, s)$ develops an $s^{1 - 2\sigma_2} = s^{-1/4}$ blowup in $H^2_\rho(\frac{5}{8})$ as $s \to 0^+$ (hostile review `A1`). Adding the explicit closed-form $G_0$-dependent term $\hat W_0 := \pi c_0(G_0)\Phi_*$ ($\hat h_0(u) = \pi c_0(G_0)\frac{u}{u^2+1}$, no shooting or iteration!) cancels the $s \to 0^+$ singularity identically while leaving the leading $\sqrt{v}$ cusp amplitude $D(0) = -\varepsilon a(G_0)$ untouched (in fact $\int_0^v y^{-1/2}\Phi_*(y)\,dy$ is flat at $v = 0^+$!). As in Track B (`A5`), working in $\mathcal{X}_\rho$ imposes the inflow boundary condition $G(0,\tau) = 0$.

### Theorem 2 (Closed Analytic A Priori Energy Bound for the $\sqrt{v}$ $\Theta$-Cusp Weak-Edge Manifold)
Let $\rho_0 \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2}]$, $a := \frac{\rho_0}{4}$, and $\rho(s) := \rho_0 - a s \in [\frac{1}{2}\rho_0, \rho_0]$ on $s \in [0, 2]$. There exist $\varepsilon_0 = \varepsilon_0(\rho_0) > 0$ and $C_0 = C_0(\rho_0) > 0$ such that for every $G_0 \in \mathcal{X}_{\rho_0}$ with $\|G_0\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho_0}} \le 1$ and every $0 < \varepsilon \le \varepsilon_0$, any smooth solution $(\widetilde G, W)$ of the autonomous $s$-clock system (2.4) on $[0, 2]$ with initial data $(\varepsilon G_0, \varepsilon^2\pi c_0(G_0)\Phi_*)$ satisfies:
1. **(Closed analytic energy bootstrap):**
   $$\sup_{s \in [0, 2]}\|\widetilde G(s)\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 + a\int_0^2 \|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G(s)\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2\,ds + \int_0^2 \|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W(s)\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2\,ds \le 2 C_0 \varepsilon^2, \tag{1.3}$$
   with zero $s \to 0^+$ blowup. *(Scope: Lemma A3 below proves this closed analytic a priori energy bound on the strictly positive strip $\rho(s) \ge \frac{1}{2}\rho_0$, replacing the broken Nishida cone norm of `270b26e`. Constructing a structure-preserving parabolic approximation and proving uniqueness via the lower-order analytic energy difference remain follow-up steps analogous to Track C Lemma C5.)*
2. **(Exact global weak-edge compatibility):** Reconstructing $\widetilde H$ from $\widetilde H_s = -(Q\widetilde G)(W - \frac{1}{2}Q\widetilde G)$ preserves $a(\tau) + D(\tau) \equiv 0$ for all $\tau \ge 0$, with exact cusp amplitude
   $$D(\tau) = -a(\tau) = \frac{-r(s(\tau))\,\varepsilon a(G_0)}{1 + \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon a(G_0)\,s(\tau)}$$
   (since $a + D \equiv 0$ and the edge limit of the $\eta$-transport equation give $\tilde D' = \frac{1}{2}\tilde D^2$, verified in `WeakEdgeManifolds.hasDerivAt_scalarEdgeD`).
3. **(Transversal horizon crossing):** Along any solution satisfying (1.3), $dr/ds = -1 + \frac{1}{2}V_{\widetilde G}(r^2)$ ($r(0)=1$) crosses $0$ transversally at a unique $s_\infty = 1 + O(\varepsilon) \in (\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2}) \subset [0, 2]$, yielding $G(v,\tau) = \widetilde G(\lambda(\tau)v, s(\tau))$ on $\tau \in [0, \infty)$ with $\|G(\cdot, \tau)\|_{L^2_\sigma} \le C\varepsilon e^{-2\sigma\tau}$ for all $\sigma \in [\frac{1}{4}, \frac{5}{8}]$ and $\tau \ge 0$.

## 2. Complete Proof (Lemmas A1–A4)

### Lemma A1 ($Q$-Decomposition, Zero-Derivative $a(\mathcal{P}(G))$, and Vanishing of $a((\frac{1}{2}+D)f)$)
1. **Pole-subtracted $Q$-smoothing (`B1`):** For any $G \in \mathcal{X}_\rho$ (even with $a(G) \neq 0$), decompose $QG = a(G) + \widetilde Q G$. Since $|a(G)| \le C_0\|G\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}$, $QG \in H^2_\rho(-\frac{1}{4})$, and $\widetilde Q G \in H^2_\rho(+\frac{1}{8})$ (with $D(QG) = D(\widetilde Q G) \in H^2_\rho(-\frac{1}{4}) \cap H^2_\rho(+\frac{1}{8})$), Cauchy–Schwarz yields
   $$\|QG\|_{H^\infty(\mathbb{S}_\rho)} \le C_Q \|G\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}, \qquad \|\mathcal{P}(G_1) - \mathcal{P}(G_2)\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho'}} \le \frac{C_{\mathcal{P}}}{\rho - \rho'}\bigl(\|G_1\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho} + \|G_2\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}\bigr)\|G_1 - G_2\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}.$$
2. **Zero-derivative IBP for $a(\mathcal{P}(G))$ (`B2`):** Even when $a(G) \neq 0$, $G(w) = O(w^{5/8})$ and $(QG)(w) = O(1)$ at $w \to 0^+$, so $w^{-1/2}(QG)G = O(w^{1/8}) \to 0$ at $w = 0$ (and $O(w^{-1/2}) \to 0$ at $w = \infty$). Integrating by parts gives the exact identity
   $$a(\mathcal{P}(G)) = -\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}} e^{-z/2} D(QG)(e^z)\,G(e^z)\,dz, \qquad \bigl|a(\mathcal{P}(G_1)) - a(\mathcal{P}(G_2))\bigr| \le C_a(\|G_1\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho} + \|G_2\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho})\|G_1 - G_2\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}. \tag{2.1}$$
3. **Zero-derivative identity $a((\frac{1}{2}+D)f) = a(f)$ (resolving `A2` for Term A!):** For **every** $f \in \mathcal{X}_\rho$ with $D f \in \mathcal{X}_{\rho'}$, integrating $\int_0^\infty w^{-1/2} f'\,dw = +\frac{1}{2}\int_0^\infty w^{-3/2} f\,dw$ by parts gives
   $$a\!\left(\left(\frac{1}{2} + D\right) f\right) = -\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\infty w^{-3/2}\left(\frac{1}{2}f + w f'\right)dw = -\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\infty w^{-3/2} f(w)\,dw = a(f). \tag{2.2}$$
   **Consequence:** For Term A, $U := -(\frac{1}{2}+D)((Q\hat G)\hat W)$, we have $a(U) = -a((Q\hat G)\hat W)$, which loses **zero derivatives** ($|a(U)| \le C\|\hat G\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}\|\hat W\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}$)! Therefore, by Lemma 2 of Track B (`theorem_track_b_pure_step_weak_edge.md`), the $t^{-1/4}|a(U)|$ term on $\operatorname{Re} m = \frac{5}{8}$ is **integrable with zero derivative loss**:
   $$\boxed{\left\|S_g(t)\left(\frac{1}{2}+D\right)\bigl((Q\hat G)\hat W\bigr)\right\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho} \le \frac{C_A}{\rho' - \rho}\|\hat G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho'}}\|\hat W\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho'}} + C_*'\,t^{-1/4}\|\hat G\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}\|\hat W\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}} \tag{2.3}$$
   for all $t > 0$ on **both** $\sigma_1 = \frac{1}{4}$ and $\sigma_2 = \frac{5}{8}$!

### Lemma A2 (Exact Cancellation of the $s \to 0^+$ Singularity & $L^2_s$ Maximal Regularity for $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{rem}}$, resolving `A1` & S1)
In the conjugated $s$-clock system ($\widetilde G = \varepsilon\hat G$, $W = \varepsilon^2\hat W$):
$$\begin{cases}
\Phi_G(\hat G, \hat W)(s) := G_0 + \varepsilon\displaystyle\int_0^s \bigl[\hat W(s') + \mathcal{P}(\hat G(s'))\bigr]\,ds', \\[10pt]
\hat W(s) = S_g(s)\hat W_0 - \varepsilon\displaystyle\int_0^s S_g(s - s')\left(\frac{1}{2}+D\right)\bigl((Q\hat G)\hat W\bigr)\,ds' + \int_0^s S_{r\to g}(s - s')\,\mathcal{R}(\hat G(s'))\,ds',
\end{cases} \tag{2.4}$$
where $\mathcal{N}_r$ is the Mellin multiplier $N(m)$ realised on the line $\operatorname{Re} m = -\frac14$ (so $\frac12 Q(\mathcal{N}_g h) = -\mathcal{N}_r(u h)$, as in `findings_session_2026_11_21_pure_step_nonlinear_manifold.md`), $S_{r\to g}(t)F := \mathcal{N}_g\bigl(e^{-ut}\,\mathcal{N}_r^{-1}F\bigr)$ (this is what `TA-4` integrates), $\mathcal{R}_\rho$ below denotes the image space $H^2_\rho(-\frac14) \cap H^2_\rho(\frac18)$ of $Q$ on $\mathcal{X}_\rho$, and $\mathcal{R}(\hat G) := \frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{2}+D)((Q\hat G)^2) + \frac{1}{2}Q(\mathcal{P}(\hat G))$ has edge value $c_0(\hat G) := \mathcal{R}(\hat G)(0) = \frac{1}{4}a(\hat G)^2 + \frac{1}{2}a(\mathcal{P}(\hat G))$. Notice that $Q(\mathcal{P}(\hat G))$ loses **zero derivatives** on $\hat G$ because $(Q\hat G)D\hat G = D((Q\hat G)\hat G) - D(Q\hat G)\hat G$ and $Q\circ D$ is order $0$ (bounded on $\mathcal{X}_\rho \to \mathcal{R}_\rho$).

Split $f_{\mathcal{R}}(u, s') := (\mathcal{N}_r^{-1}\mathcal{R}(\hat G(s')))(u) = \pi c_0(\hat G(s'))\,\frac{u^2}{u^2+1} + \tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}(u, s')$:
1. **Pole-free remainder $\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}$ via $L^2_s$ maximal regularity (resolving S1):** Subtracting $\pi c_0(\hat G)\frac{u^2}{u^2+1}$ cancels the $m = 0$ pole of $\widehat{\mathcal{R}}/N$ identically, so $\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}$ is holomorphic on $-\frac{1}{4} \le \operatorname{Re} m \le +\frac{1}{8}$. Although $T[f](z,s) := \int_0^s u(z)e^{-u(z)(s-s')}f(z,s')\,ds'$ is unbounded from $L^\infty_s L^2_x \to L^2_x$, it is **uniformly bounded on $L^2_s([0, s_0]; L^2_x)$** by Cauchy–Schwarz and Fubini:
   $$\int_0^{s_0} |T[f](z,s)|^2\,ds \le \int_0^{s_0}\!\left(\int_0^s |u|e^{-\operatorname{Re}(u)(s-s')}\,ds'\right)\!\left(\int_0^s |u|e^{-\operatorname{Re}(u)(s-s')}|f(z,s')|^2\,ds'\right)ds \le \frac{1}{\cos^2(\rho/2)}\int_0^{s_0} |f(z,s')|^2\,ds'.$$
   The identical bound holds for all $\partial_z^k(u e^{-u(s-s')})$, so $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{rem}}[\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}](s) := \mathcal{N}_g[\int_0^s e^{-u(s-s')}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}(u,s')\,ds']$ satisfies the **$L^2_s$-in-time** 1-derivative bound for all $0 < \rho' < \rho$ and $s_0 \in (0, 2]$:
   $$\bigl\|\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{rem}}[\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}(\hat G_1) - \tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}(\hat G_2)]\bigr\|_{L^2([0,s_0];\mathcal{X}_{\rho'})} \le \frac{C_{\mathrm{rem}}}{\rho - \rho'}\bigl\|\hat G_1 - \hat G_2\bigr\|_{L^2([0,s_0];\mathcal{X}_\rho)}\sup_{s'\le s_0}\bigl(\|\hat G_1\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho} + \|\hat G_2\|_{\mathcal{X}_\rho}\bigr). \tag{2.5}$$
2. **Modified algebraic fixed-point map $\widetilde\Phi_W$ (resolving S2 #2):** On any $C^1$-in-$s$ trajectory $\hat G(s)$, integrating by parts in $s'$ gives the exact identity
   $$\pi c_0(G_0)\phi_*(u)\,e^{-u s} + \int_0^s \pi u\,\phi_*(u)\,e^{-u(s-s')}\,c_0(\hat G(s'))\,ds' = \pi c_0(\hat G(s))\,\phi_*(u) - \pi\phi_*(u)\int_0^s e^{-u(s-s')}\,\frac{d}{ds'}\bigl[c_0(\hat G(s'))\bigr]\,ds'. \tag{2.6}$$
   Define the **algebraic** scalar functional
   $$\dot c_0(\hat G, \hat W) := \frac{\varepsilon}{2}a(\hat G)\bigl(a(\hat W) + a(\mathcal{P}(\hat G))\bigr) + \frac{\varepsilon}{2}\,D[a\circ\mathcal{P}](\hat G)\cdot\bigl(\hat W + \mathcal{P}(\hat G)\bigr), \tag{2.7}$$
   and define the **modified fixed-point map** $\widetilde\Phi_W(\hat G, \hat W)(s)$ by replacing the rank-1 integral + $S_g(s)\hat W_0$ in (2.4) with $\pi c_0(\hat G(s))\Phi_* - \pi\int_0^s \mathcal{N}_g[e^{-u(s-s')}\phi_*]\,\dot c_0(\hat G(s'), \hat W(s'))\,ds'$. Then $\widetilde\Phi_W$ requires **no** $s$-differentiability of its input $(\hat G, \hat W)$, and at any fixed point $(\hat G, \hat W) = (\Phi_G, \widetilde\Phi_W)(\hat G, \hat W)$ we have $\hat G_s = \varepsilon(\hat W + \mathcal{P}(\hat G))$ (since $\hat W, \mathcal{P}(\hat G) \in L^2_s$), so $\dot c_0(\hat G, \hat W) \equiv \frac{d}{ds}[c_0(\hat G(s))]$ and undoing (2.6) recovers the exact Duhamel equation (2.4)!

### Lemma A3 (Time-Dependent Analytic Energy Closure on $\rho(s) = \rho_0 - a s$, replacing the Nishida Cone Norm)
Fix $a := \frac{\rho_0}{4}$ so $\rho(s) := \rho_0 - a s \in [\rho_1, \rho_0]$ ($\rho_1 = \frac{1}{2}\rho_0 > 0$) remains strictly positive on all of $s \in [0, 2]$ (eliminating the $d_a(\rho, s) \to 0$ cone-boundary obstruction of `TA-A3 [S1]`). For any $\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ and $F(s) \in H^2_{\rho(s)}(\mu)$, differentiating $\|F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu}^2 = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{\mathbb{R}} \cosh(2\rho(s)\xi)|\widehat F(\mu + i\xi)|^2\,d\xi$ gives (since $|\xi|\sinh(2\rho|\xi|) \ge c_1(\rho_1)(1 + \xi^2)^{1/2}\cosh(2\rho|\xi|) - C_1\cosh(2\rho|\xi|)$ for $\rho \ge \rho_1$):
$$\frac{d}{ds}\|F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu}^2 + 2 a c_1(\rho_1)\|\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu}^2 \le 2 C_1 a\|F\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu}^2 + 2\operatorname{Re}\langle F_s, F\rangle_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu}. \tag{2.8}$$
Write $W = \mathcal{N}_g h$ and split $h = h_{\mathrm{alg}} + h_{\mathrm{rk1}} + h_{\mathrm{reg}}$, where:
- $h_{\mathrm{alg}}(u, s) := \pi c_0(\widetilde G(s))\,\phi_*(u)$ ($W_{\mathrm{alg}} := \mathcal{N}_g h_{\mathrm{alg}} = \pi c_0(\widetilde G(s))\Phi_* \in \mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}$, cancelling the $m = 0$ pole of $f_{\mathcal{R}}$ identically via $u h_{\mathrm{alg}} = \pi c_0(\widetilde G)\frac{u^2}{u^2+1}$ and giving $h_{\mathrm{reg}}(\cdot, 0) \equiv 0$);
- $\beta(s') := \pi\dot c_0(\widetilde G, W)(s') - \frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W)(s')$ and $h_{\mathrm{rk1}}(u, s) := -\int_0^s e^{-u(s - s')}\beta(s')\phi_*(u)\,ds'$.
  Check the $m = \frac{1}{2}$ residue cancellation on the forcing: for $F_A := -(\frac{1}{2}+D)((Q\widetilde G)W)$, Lemma A1(3) gives $a(F_A) = -a((Q\widetilde G)W)$, so $\operatorname{Res}_{m=1/2}(\widehat{F_A}/N) = +\frac{\pi}{2}a(F_A) = -\frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W)$. Subtracting $(\partial_s + u)h_{\mathrm{rk1}} = -\beta\phi_*$ adds $+\beta\phi_*$ to the RHS of $(\partial_s + u)h_{\mathrm{reg}}$: $+\pi\dot c_0\phi_*$ cancels $-\pi\dot c_0\phi_*$ from $\partial_s h_{\mathrm{alg}}$ identically, while $-\frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W)\phi_*$ has residue $(-\frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W))(-1) = +\frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W)$ at $m = \frac{1}{2}$, cancelling $\operatorname{Res}_{m=1/2}(\widehat{F_A}/N)$ identically. The energy below runs in physical space on the paired lines ($-\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{8}$ for $\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}$; $0, \frac{3}{8}$ for Term A and the rank-1 subtraction).

On each of the two lines $\mu \in \{\mu_1, \mu_2\} = \{0, \frac{3}{8}\}$ (both strictly below $m = \frac{1}{2}$, so $\phi_* \in H^\infty(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho_0}, \mu)$), define the line-dependent variable $v_\mu := \langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2} h_{\mathrm{reg}}$ (so on $\sigma = \mu + \frac{1}{4} \in \{\frac{1}{4}, \frac{5}{8}\}$, $\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}$ has Mellin symbol $(1 + (\xi - \frac{i}{4})^2)^{-1/4} \asymp \langle\xi\rangle^{-1/2}$, giving the norm equivalence $\|v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, \sigma)} \asymp \|\langle D\rangle_\sigma^{-1/2} W_{\mathrm{reg}}\|_{\sigma}$ with exact integer orders $k_1 = 2$ on $\sigma_1 = \frac{1}{4}$ and $k_2 = 3$ on $\sigma_2 = \frac{5}{8}$). Applying $\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}$ to $(\partial_s + u)h_{\mathrm{reg}}$ gives the exact equation (keeping the non-zero commutator $L_\mu := \langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2} u \langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} \neq u$!):
$$\partial_s v_\mu + L_\mu v_\mu = -\mathcal{N}_{1/4}^{-1}\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\left(\frac{1}{2}+D\right)\bigl((Q\widetilde G)W\bigr) + \langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}(\widetilde G) - \frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W)\,\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\phi_*, \qquad v_\mu(\cdot, 0) \equiv 0. \tag{2.9}$$

1. **$\widetilde G$-energy in $\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}$:** Applying (2.8) to $\widetilde G_s = W + \mathcal{P}(\widetilde G)$ on $\sigma_1 = \frac{1}{4}$ and $\sigma_2 = \frac{5}{8}$ (with $\langle D\rangle^{1/4}$ on $\sigma_2$):
   $$\frac{d}{ds}\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 + 2 a c_1\|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 \le 2 C_1 a\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 + \frac{a c_1}{2}\|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 + \frac{2}{a c_1}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 + C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2.$$
2. **Exact-symbol complex-strip hierarchical $u$-coercivity with commutator $L_\mu = \langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2} u \langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2}$:** Because $\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}$ is self-adjoint on $H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, \mu)$,
   $$\operatorname{Re}\langle L_\mu v_\mu, v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, \mu)} = \operatorname{Re}\langle u\,\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu,\, \langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2} v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_\rho, \mu)}.$$
   Conjugating by $e^{-\sigma x}$ ($g := e^{-\sigma x} v_\mu$, $\sigma = \mu + \frac{1}{4}$) turns the right-hand side into a constant-coefficient Fourier multiplier in $\xi$ with exact symbol
   $$S_{k,\rho}(\xi) := \operatorname{Re}\!\left[e^{\mp i\rho/2}\,\phi_{\mathrm{comm}}(\xi)\,\sum_{m=0}^k c_m^{(k)}\bigl(\xi^2 - \tfrac{1}{16} + \tfrac{i}{2}\xi\bigr)^m\right], \qquad \phi_{\mathrm{comm}}(\xi) := \frac{(1 + (\xi - \frac{i}{4})^2)^{1/4}}{\overline{(1 + (\xi - \frac{i}{4})^2)^{1/4}}},$$
   where $|\phi_{\mathrm{comm}}(\xi)| \equiv 1$ identically and $\max_{\xi\in\mathbb{R}}|\arg\phi_{\mathrm{comm}}(\xi)| = \frac{1}{2}\arctan(1/\sqrt{15}) = 7.243^\circ$. With geometric weights $c_m^{(k)} = B^{k-m}$, the ratio $S_{k,\rho}(\xi) / \sum_{m=0}^k c_m^{(k)}\xi^{2m}$ stays strictly positive uniformly over all $|\rho| \le \frac{\pi}{2}$ and all $\xi \in \mathbb{R}$ (`TA-5`: minimum ratio $\ge 0.6027 > 0.5$ for $k=2, B=6$ and $\ge 0.5321 > 0.5$ for $k=3, B=8$). Hence (with norm-equivalence constant $C_{\mathrm{eq}} > 0$):
   $$\operatorname{Re}\langle L_\mu v_\mu, v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu)} \ge c_u'\,\|v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \sigma)}^2 \ge \frac{c_u'}{C_{\mathrm{eq}}}\,\|\langle D\rangle_\sigma^{-1/2} W_{\mathrm{reg}}\|_{\sigma,\rho(s)}^2 \qquad \bigl((\mu, \sigma) \in \{(0, \tfrac{1}{4}), (\tfrac{3}{8}, \tfrac{5}{8})\}\bigr). \tag{2.10}$$
3. **Pairing the RHS of (2.9) against $v_\mu$ in $H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu)$:**
   - **Pole-free source $\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}(\widetilde G)$:** Using $e^{\pm x/4}\partial_x^m(e^{\mp x/4} g) = (\partial_x \mp \frac{1}{4})^m g$ ($m = 0..k_\sigma$) and Cauchy–Schwarz gives
     $$\bigl|\langle \langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}, v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}\bigr| \le \frac{c_u'}{4}\|v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\sigma)}^2 + C\|\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}\|_{H^{k_\sigma}(\sigma - 1/2)}^2.$$
     Since $\mathcal{N}_r^{-1}$ has order $-1$ on $\sigma_1 - \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{1}{4}$ and $-\frac{7}{4}$ on $\sigma_2 - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$, $\|\langle D\rangle_0^{-1/2}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}\|_{H^2(-1/4)} + \|\langle D\rangle_{3/8}^{-1/2}\tilde f_{\mathcal{R}}\|_{H^3(1/8)} \asymp \|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, -1/4} + \|\langle D\rangle^{3/4}\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}\|_{\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, 1/8} \le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}$.
   - **Rank-1 subtraction $-\frac{\pi}{2}a((Q\widetilde G)W)\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}\phi_*$:** Since $\phi_* \in H^\infty(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho_0}, \mu)$ for $\mu \in \{0, \frac{3}{8}\}$ and $|a((Q\widetilde G)W)| \le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}$, its pairing with $v_\mu$ is bounded by $C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}(\mu)}$.
   - **Term A** ($-\mathcal{N}_{1/4}^{-1}\langle D\rangle_\mu^{-1/2}(\frac{1}{2}+D)((Q\widetilde G)W)$): Decompose $Q\widetilde G = a(\widetilde G)\chi(z) + r(z)$ where $\chi(z) := (1 + e^z)^{-1} \in C^\infty_b(\mathbb{S}_{\rho_0})$ ($\rho_0 \le \frac{\pi}{2} < \pi$) and $\|r\|_{H^1(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)})} \le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}$; thus multiplication by $Q\widetilde G$ is bounded on $H^{1/2}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)})$ and $H^{3/4}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)})$ with norm $\le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}$. Splitting $W = \mathcal{N}_g\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu + W_{\mathrm{alg}} + W_{\mathrm{rk1}}$ on the zero-free/pole-free lines $\mu_1 = 0$ ($\mathcal{N}_g$ order $\frac{3}{2}$) and $\mu_2 = \frac{3}{8}$ ($\mathcal{N}_g$ order $\frac{9}{4}$):
     - *Principal piece $W_{\mathrm{reg}} = \mathcal{N}_g\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu$:* exact derivative match on both lines ($\frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 2 + \frac{1}{2}$ on $\mu_1 = 0$, $\frac{9}{4} + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{4} = 3 + \frac{1}{2}$ on $\mu_2 = \frac{3}{8}$), giving $|\langle \text{Term A}_{\mathrm{reg}}, v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}| \le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}^2$.
     - *Algebraic piece $W_{\mathrm{alg}} = \pi c_0(\widetilde G)\Phi_*$:* since $\Phi_* \in H^\infty(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho_0}, \mu)$ on $\mu \in \{0, \frac{3}{8}\}$, $|\langle \text{Term A}_{\mathrm{alg}}, v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}| \le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^3\|\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}$.
     - *Rank-1 piece $W_{\mathrm{rk1}} = \mathcal{N}_g h_{\mathrm{rk1}}$:* since $\mu < \frac{1}{2}$, $\|e^{-ut}\phi_*\|_{H^k(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho_0}, \mu)} \le C_k$ **uniformly** in $t \ge 0$, so $\|W_{\mathrm{rk1}}(s)\|_{H^{3/4}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, \mu)} \le C\|\beta\|_{L^1([0,2])}$ uniformly in $s \in [0, 2]$, giving $|\langle \text{Term A}_{\mathrm{rk1}}, v_\mu\rangle_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}| \le C\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}\|\beta\|_{L^1([0,2])}\|\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu\|_{H^{k_\sigma}_{\mathrm{hier}}(\mu)}$.
   - **$L^2_s$ bound on $W_{\mathrm{rk1}}$ in $\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}$:** Using $t^{-1/4} \in L^2([0, 2])$ ($\int_0^2 t^{-1/2}dt = 2\sqrt{2}$) with Young's $L^2 * L^1 \to L^2$ convolution inequality (pairing $D[a \circ \mathcal{P}](\widetilde G)\cdot W$ in $H^{1/2} \times H^{-1/2}$):
     $$\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W_{\mathrm{rk1}}\|_{L^2([0,2]; \mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)})} \le C\|\beta\|_{L^1([0,2])} \le C'\!\left(\sup_s\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^3 + \sup_s\|\widetilde G\|\|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|_{L^2_s}^2 + \|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|_{L^2_s}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W\|_{L^2_s}\right),$$
     while $\|W_{\mathrm{alg}}\|_{L^2([0,2];\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)})} \le C\sup_s\|\widetilde G\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2$.
4. **Closed integrated bootstrap on $[0, 2]$:** Define $\mathcal{E}(s) := \|\widetilde G(s)\|_{\mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}}^2 + \gamma\bigl(\|v_0(s)\|_{H^2_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, 0)}^2 + \|v_{3/8}(s)\|_{H^3_{\mathrm{hier}}(\partial\mathbb{S}_{\rho(s)}, 3/8)}^2\bigr)$ with $\gamma := \frac{24 C_{\mathrm{eq}}}{a c_1 c_u'}$ (so the remaining dissipation $\frac{\gamma c_u'}{2 C_{\mathrm{eq}}} = \frac{12}{a c_1}$ leaves a full $\frac{6}{a c_1}$ positive margin after absorbing $\frac{6}{a c_1}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W_{\mathrm{reg}}\|^2$ from the $\widetilde G$-equation!). Multiply the combined energy inequality by $e^{-2 C_1 a s}$ and integrate on $[0, s]$ ($s \in [0, 2]$). Under the bootstrap hypothesis $\sup_{s\le 2}\mathcal{E}(s) + a\int_0^2 \|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|^2 ds + \int_0^2 \|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W\|^2 ds \le 4 C_0 \varepsilon^2$, we have $\|\beta\|_{L^1([0,2])} = O(\varepsilon^2 / \sqrt{a})$ and $\frac{6}{a c_1}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W_{\mathrm{rk1}}\|_{L^2_s}^2 = O(\varepsilon^4 / a^2) \ll \varepsilon^2$, while all $O(\varepsilon)$ dissipation terms are absorbed by $+a c_1\|\langle D\rangle^{1/2}\widetilde G\|^2 + \gamma a c_1\sum_\mu\|\langle D\rangle_\mu^{1/2} v_\mu\|^2 + \frac{6}{a c_1}\|\langle D\rangle^{-1/2} W_{\mathrm{reg}}\|^2$ for $0 < \varepsilon \le \varepsilon_0(\rho_0)$, closing the *a priori* energy bound (1.3)!

**Reconstruction of $\widetilde H$ and exact compatibility $a(\tau) + D(\tau) \equiv 0$:** Along any smooth solution $(\widetilde G, W)$ satisfying (1.3), define $\widetilde H(w,s)$ by integrating $\widetilde H_s = -(Q\widetilde G)(W - \frac{1}{2}Q\widetilde G)$ from $\widetilde H(\cdot,0) = (\frac{1}{2}+D)^{-1}(W_0 - \frac{1}{2}Q(\varepsilon G_0))$. The residual $E := (\frac{1}{2}+D)\widetilde H + \frac{1}{2}Q\widetilde G - W$ satisfies $E(\cdot,0) = 0$ and $E_s \equiv 0$ by (2.4), so $E \equiv 0$ for all $s \in [0, 2]$. Evaluating $E = 0$ at $w = 0$ (where $W(0,s) \equiv 0$ since $W(\cdot,s) \in \mathcal{X}_{\rho(s)}$) yields $\frac{1}{2}\widetilde H(0,s) + \frac{1}{2}(Q\widetilde G)(0,s) \equiv 0$, i.e. $\tilde a(s) + \widetilde D(s) \equiv 0$, and hence $a(\tau) + D(\tau) = r(s(\tau))(\tilde a(s) + \widetilde D(s)) \equiv 0$ for all $\tau \ge 0$.

### Lemma A4 (Horizon Crossing & Physical-Time Reconstruction along Solutions of (1.3))
For $G \in \mathcal{X}_{\rho_1}$ with $a(G) \neq 0$, $V_G(r^2) = r\,a(G) + O(r^{5/4})$ (`B4`), which is $C^1$ on $r \ge 0$ with $\partial_r V_{\widetilde G}(r^2) = a(\widetilde G(s)) + O(\varepsilon r^{1/4}) = O(\varepsilon)$. Hence along any smooth solution satisfying the *a priori* bound (1.3), $dr/ds = -1 + \frac{1}{2}V_{\widetilde G}(r^2) \le -\frac{1}{2}$ hits $0$ transversally at a unique $s_\infty = 1 + O(\varepsilon) \in (\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2}) \subset [0, 2]$, yielding $(G, \eta)$ on $\tau \in [0, \infty)$ with $a(\tau) + D(\tau) \equiv 0$ and $\|G(\cdot,\tau)\|_{L^2_\sigma} \le C\varepsilon e^{-2\sigma\tau}$. $\blacksquare$
